Our hub has been passionate about gaming for as long as we can remember. We’ve experienced countless titles, exploring everything from the iconic Prince of Persia—a predecessor that inspired the legendary Assassin’s Creed series—to Project I.G.I., a game that laid the groundwork for modern first-person shooters.

Here, you’ll discover some of the achievements we hold closest to our hearts. But truthfully, it’s not the trophies themselves that matter. What truly counts is the journey—the countless hours spent grinding, immersing ourselves in rich, imaginative worlds, and forming lasting connections and friendships, both real and fictional.

Gaming, much like any other form of entertainment, is undeniably a form of art. We take pride in being part of this creative landscape, regardless of how significant or minor our accomplishments may seem. It’s about being more than just players—it’s about being artists, contributors to a digital renaissance we’re thrilled to be part of.
